

# 🍽️ NutriGen - AI-Powered Meal Planner

## Vision
A Gen Z-focused meal planning app that uses AI to scan your pantry, understand your health goals, and serve up personalized meal suggestions that fit your lifestyle.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation & First Impressions

### 1.1 Splash Screen
- Animated logo reveal (0.3s)
- Bold gradient background with smooth fade-in
- Brand identity establishment

### 1.2 Trial Popup
- Clean modal on first launch
- "Free for 20 days" messaging with countdown
- Skip option + Start Trial CTA

---

## Phase 2: Onboarding Flow (4-5 screens max)

### 2.1 Welcome & Account
- Quick sign-up (email or social login)
- Catchy tagline: "Eat smarter, not harder"

### 2.2 Health Profile
- Goal selection (lose weight, maintain, gain muscle, eat healthier)
- Daily calorie target (auto-suggested based on goal)
- Any dietary restrictions/allergies

### 2.3 Meal Time Preferences
- Preferred breakfast, lunch, dinner times
- Snack preferences (yes/no, how many)
- Weekend vs weekday flexibility

### 2.4 Pantry Setup Options
- Three ways to add pantry items:
  - 📝 **Text**: Quick typing with autocomplete
  - 📸 **Photo**: Snap a photo, AI detects items
  - 🎥 **Video**: Scan your fridge/pantry in real-time
- Skip option (can add later)

---

## Phase 3: Dashboard & Core Experience

### 3.1 Personalized Dashboard
- Daily greeting with motivational content
- Today's meal plan at a glance (breakfast → lunch → dinner)
- Calorie progress ring
- Quick action buttons (Add meal, Skip meal, Surprise me)
- Health streak/progress widget

### 3.2 Meal Cards
- Beautiful food imagery
- Calories per serving displayed prominently
- Prep time, difficulty level
- Ingredients from your pantry highlighted
- Swap/Skip/Save options

### 3.3 Meal Suggestions
- AI suggests meals based on:
  - Time of day & your schedule
  - Available pantry items
  - Calorie goals & health profile
  - Past preferences & ratings
- "Cook this" or "Suggest another" buttons

---

## Phase 4: Pantry Management

### 4.1 Smart Pantry
- Grid view of all items with expiry tracking
- Three input methods:
  - Text with autocomplete
  - Photo capture with AI detection
  - Video scanning for bulk add
- Quick-add from recipes

### 4.2 AI Detection (Photo/Video)
- Real-time item recognition
- Confidence indicators
- Manual correction option
- Quantity estimation

---

## Phase 5: Health & Progress Tracking

### 5.1 Health Dashboard
- Weekly/monthly calorie trends
- Macro breakdown (proteins, carbs, fats)
- Meal consistency tracking
- Goal progress visualization

### 5.2 Smart Notifications & Reminders
- Meal time reminders
- "Time to eat!" with today's suggestion
- Weekly progress summaries
- Motivational nudges

---

## Phase 6: Settings & Personalization

### 6.1 Theme Switcher
- Bold & Vibrant (default)
- Dark mode option
- Smooth transition animations

### 6.2 Profile Management
- Edit dietary preferences
- Update meal times
- Notification settings
- Account & subscription management

---

## Design Principles

| Aspect | Approach |
|--------|----------|
| **Colors** | Vibrant gradients, bold accents |
| **Typography** | Modern, rounded sans-serif |
| **Icons** | Playful, recognizable |
| **Animations** | Smooth 0.2-0.3s transitions |
| **Cards** | Rounded corners, subtle shadows |
| **Mobile** | Thumb-friendly bottom navigation |

---

## Technical Requirements

- **Backend**: Lovable Cloud for user accounts, meal data, and preferences
- **AI**: Lovable AI for pantry detection and meal suggestions
- **Storage**: Image storage for pantry photos and recipe images
- **Notifications**: Push notification system for reminders
